› The Member Experience

How we build elite case solvers.

Our progressive 4-step framework takes members from classroom theory to championship podiums and high-tier careers.

Stage 01 • Onboarding 01

Foundations, MECE & Analytical Toolkits

Every member learns structured consulting methodologies. We break complex, ambiguous business problems into mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive components.

Tool 01 Issue Trees, Hypothesis-driven problem solving & Root Cause Analysis
Tool 02 Financial unit economics, market sizing & valuation fundamentals
Stage 02 • Training 02

Real-Time Case Sprints with Corporate Partners

Put skills to the test under timed conditions (3 hours to 24 hours). Teams dissect strategic market entries, digital transformations, and M&A opportunities based on real business cases.

Format 4-person multidisciplinary student teams working under tight deadlines
Insight Direct mentorship from senior students and industry alumni
Stage 03 • Presentation 03

Executive Slide Decks & Persuasive Pitching

A great strategy is only as powerful as your ability to communicate it. We train members in crisp slide design, executive presence, and defending recommendations in live Q&A panels.

Skill 01 Top-down communication & Minto Pyramid principle
Skill 02 10-minute presentation drill + 10-minute rigorous jury defense
Stage 04 • Impact 04

Championship Podiums & Career Acceleration

Selected delegation teams travel to national and Nordic competitions. Our alumni leverage their proven track record to secure top roles in strategy consulting, investment banking, and management.

Podium 1st Place Nordic Case Challenge 2025 • 2nd Place Mowi Case Cup 2025
Network Lifelong connections with ambitious peers and business leaders
› About Us

Bridging business and academia to shape tomorrow's leaders.

UiA Case Club was founded in 2024 by students at the University of Agder School of Business and Law in Kristiansand. We are dedicated to creating a vibrant, ambitious case-solving environment where curious minds build practical strategy skills, solve authentic business dilemmas, and develop lasting professional confidence.
