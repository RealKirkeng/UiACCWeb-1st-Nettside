› Methodology

The Four Core Pillars

Our development model focuses on building structured thinkers who can solve any business dilemma under pressure.

01

Structured Case Training

Weekly practice sessions covering market entry, strategy, operations, and M&A cases, using frameworks adopted by McKinsey, BCG, and Bain.

02

Presentation & Storytelling

Constructing professional storyboards and high-impact slide designs while training in executive presence to pitch persuasively to corporate juries.

03

National Competition Prep

Dedicated practice rounds, internal selection cycles, and coaching sessions from experienced strategy consultants to win Norway's top case cups.

04

International Ambitions

Representing the University of Agder globally, competing head-to-head with the Nordic and European business schools on international stages.

› Roadmap

Our Training Curriculum

Our semester-long training program prepares students step-by-step for high-stakes competition.

Weeks 1 - 4

Phase 1: Analytical Foundations

We begin with issue trees, MECE problem structuring, hypothesis testing, and the Pyramidal Principle. Members learn to break down any complex market issue into logical, structured branches.

Weeks 5 - 8

Phase 2: Deep Dive Case Solving

Focusing on corporate valuations, market entry strategy, cost optimization, operations, and synergy modeling. Members work in groups to solve mock cases from premier consultancy frameworks.

Weeks 9 - 12

Phase 3: Presentation Masterclass & Sprints

Storyboarding slide decks, slide composition techniques, and high-impact verbal delivery. Members undergo mock case sprints, presenting directly to a jury of alumni and industry executives.
