› FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applications, background requirements, and case solving workloads.

We accept applications from all active students enrolled at the University of Agder, especially those at the School of Business and Law. There are no specific degree requirements—we value diverse backgrounds, including engineering, economics, marketing, and computer science.

No prior experience is necessary. We train all our selected members from the ground up. You will learn core problem-solving frameworks (MECE, Pyramidal Principle), quantitative modeling, spreadsheet analysis, slide storyboarding, and public presentation skills.

All members are expected to attend our weekly 2-hour training session (normally held in the evening). During competition phases, teams preparing for national cups or global tournaments will dedicate additional hours as a group to mock cases, deck structuring, and presentation runs.
