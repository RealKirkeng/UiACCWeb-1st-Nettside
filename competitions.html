› The Competition Format

What is a Case Competition?

A business case competition is a high-pressure academic event where student teams are given a real corporate challenge—with no prior knowledge of the company or industry—and are tasked with generating a comprehensive solution within a strict timeframe (typically 24 hours).

Teams must analyze datasets, formulate a corporate strategy, construct a financial valuation spreadsheet, storyboard a slide deck, and pitch their recommendation directly to a panel of corporate executives, board directors, and senior advisors. It is the ultimate test of business acumen, teamwork, and composure.

The Challenge Dynamics:

  • • Time constraints: Normally 3 to 24-hour sprints. No external assistance allowed.
  • • Data sets: Financial filings, market reports, competitor insights, and internal company details.
  • • Pitch session: 15-minute deck pitch followed by intense Q&A from C-level executives.
