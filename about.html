About UiA Case Club

Developing Agder's premier strategic thinkers, analysts, and presenters through rigorous problem solving and strong industry ties.

› Vision & Mission
Our Slogan

Connect student and business.

We connect students with the business community, providing practical experience and establishing valuable networks with potential employers.

Our Vision

Becoming Norway's leading case community

We aim to establish Kristiansand and UiA as the premier Northern European hub for case solving, cultivating a culture of academic rigor and structured problem-solving.

Our Mission

Making our members the best version of themselves

By providing world-class training in analysis, modeling, and public speaking, we prepare members to excel at global case competitions and top-tier professional roles.

› Our History

Where ambition meets expertise.

Founded in 2024 at the University of Agder's School of Business and Law in Kristiansand, UiA Case Club is a student-driven organization built for those who want to bridge academic theory with real business challenges. We practice structured problem-solving, analytical frameworks, and high-stakes presentation polish.

Our members represent UiA at national and international business case competitions. Through weekly workshops, corporate Case Nights, and mentorship from industry alumni, we build a community that cultivates accelerated career growth.
